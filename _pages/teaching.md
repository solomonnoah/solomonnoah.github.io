## Directed Reading Program
Along with Liza Ter-Saakov, I organize the [directed reading program at Georgia Tech](https://math.gatech.edu/directed-reading-program). This program pairs undergraduates with graudate students and postdocs to spend a semester reading about a topic they would otherwise not encounter in their curriculum. We also work to provide professional development and community events throughout the semester. In addition to running the program, I have also mentored students on topics including:
 - Chip firing games on finite graphs
 - Representations of quivers
 - The totally non-negative Grassmannian
